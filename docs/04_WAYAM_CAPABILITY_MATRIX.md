## Global rules

- **Exclusion terms (72):** reject a notice when one appears in the title and no strong capability phrase is present (e.g. civil works, toll/user fee, housekeeping, catering, tyres, lattice tower, selection of developer).
- **Technology signals (27):** keep a notice for free semantic scoring even without capability keywords (high-recall net).
- **Synonyms (28):** abbreviation expansion (AI, CCTV, ANPR, VAPT, SOC, HMIS, BESS…), counted once per concept.

Editing guidance: add phrases buyers actually use; put words that also appear in unrelated work under `weak`; record new proof (a won contract, a certification) in `company_facts` and the config `company_profile` — never in keywords. Every edit changes `kb_version`, which re-scores affected notices on the next run.
